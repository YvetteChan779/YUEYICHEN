- 项目 1：具身项目 VLN & VLA - 基于 VLA 的具身视觉跟踪与 VLN 导航基座 ABot-NO
  - 时间：2025.04 - 2025.10
  - 角色：Embodied Visual Tracking 项目负责人；ABot-NO Instruction-Goal / Object-Goal 核心贡献者

  - 中文 bullet 版
    - 负责基于 VLA 的具身视觉跟踪，在拥挤、遮挡、干扰等困难场景下，以 Qwen2.5-VL-3B + Flow Matching Head 为基座，在 SFT 模型上进一步进行多智能体对抗 RL 后训练。
    - 将遮挡和干扰显式建模为语言条件的对抗博弈：Follower 执行"持续跟随目标"，Blocker 执行"制造遮挡与重识别困难"，使跟踪模型在主动干扰下学习重识别与跟随策略。
    - 在 SFT 阶段结合仿真采集数据与 VQA 数据，以保留 VLM 先验；RL 阶段采用 GRPO + LoRA，通过组内相对优势稳定更新策略。
    - 在 EVT-Bench 达到学术界 SOTA，单人场景跟踪成功率 92.1%；原文称 3B 模型全面超越银河通用 / 字节 SEED 的 7B 竞品。
    - 以通讯作者投稿 ECCV 2026，原文标注已录用。
    - 参与高德 ABot 导航基座大模型，原文描述为 Qwen3-4B3 + Flow Matching Action Head，统一五类导航任务并真机部署于 Unitree Go2 + Orin NX。
    - 负责 Instruction-Goal、Object-Goal 两类导航任务的 Habitat 仿真训练数据采集、模型训练与仿真评测。

  - English bullet version
    - Led VLA-based embodied visual tracking for crowded and occluded scenes by post-training a Qwen2.5-VL-3B + Flow Matching Head model with multi-agent adversarial RL on top of SFT.
    - Formulated occlusion and distractor interference as a language-conditioned adversarial game, where a Follower learns to continuously track the target while a Blocker learns to create occlusion and re-identification difficulty.
    - Combined simulation-collected trajectories with VQA data during SFT to retain VLM priors, then applied GRPO + LoRA with group-relative advantages to stabilize adversarial policy updates.
    - Achieved academic SOTA on EVT-Bench with 92.1% single-person tracking success rate; reported that the 3B model outperformed 7B competitors from Galaxy General Robotics and ByteDance SEED.
    - Contributed to AutoNavi ABot-NO navigation foundation model, described as Qwen3-4B3 + Flow Matching Action Head, unifying five navigation task families and deploying on Unitree Go2 + Orin NX.
    - Owned Habitat data generation, training, and simulation evaluation for Instruction-Goal and Object-Goal navigation tasks.

- 项目 2：具身项目 MotionVLA 动作生成大模型
  - 时间：2025.11 - 2026.05
  - 角色：技术负责人；负责动作数据生成、生成模型架构设计与训练、RL/后训练、Orin 端侧部署

  - 中文 bullet 版
    - 面向四足动作数据稀缺问题，负责 Video2Motion 数据生成，微调 Wan2.2 生成机器狗动作视频。
    - 设计 Identity Consistency Loss 缓解跨帧 identity drift，并基于 CLIP 相似度等质量指标搭建多级过滤流程，最终产出 19 维机器狗关节动作。
    - 拟开源 Quad-Imaginarium 数据集，包含 7,488 个机器狗动作 / 18.5 小时数据；以通讯作者身份投稿 CoRL 2026。
    - 参考 MoT 架构设计 MotionVLA 文本-动作双模态专家架构：以 GPT-2 初始化 Transformer 骨干，文本与动作作为平级模态共享 self-attention。
    - 冻结文本分支以保持语言模型语义能力，同时在共享注意力层接入独立参数化的动作专家分支；架构末端加入 Diffusion Head，在动作隐空间进行流形扩散并直接生成 joints 数据。
    - 设计 RL 后训练方案：VLA 对同一指令采样多条候选动作，在物理仿真中 rollout，以任务执行成功率与运动稳定性为奖励，并用 GRPO 组内相对优势更新生成策略。
    - 原文报告真机执行成功率从 40% 提升至 95%；拟以第一作者投稿 AAAI 2027。
    - 通过 ONNX 转换与实时推理流水线将模型部署到 NVIDIA Orin，端到端延迟 0.5 s。

  - English bullet version
    - Led Video2Motion data generation for quadruped motion learning under severe public-data scarcity by fine-tuning Wan2.2 to synthesize robot-dog motion videos.
    - Designed an Identity Consistency Loss to reduce cross-frame identity drift and built CLIP-based multi-stage quality filtering to produce 19-D robot-dog joint actions.
    - Planned the Quad-Imaginarium dataset release with 7,488 quadruped actions and 18.5 hours of motion data; submitted to CoRL 2026 as corresponding author.
    - Architected MotionVLA as a MoT-inspired text-action dual-modal expert model, initializing the Transformer backbone from GPT-2 and treating text and action as peer modalities under shared self-attention.
    - Preserved language semantics by freezing the text branch, inserted independently parameterized action experts into shared attention layers, and added a Diffusion Head for manifold diffusion in action latent space.
    - Designed GRPO-based RL post-training where the VLA samples multiple candidate motions per instruction, rolls them out in physics simulation, and optimizes task success and motion stability with group-relative advantages.
    - Reported real-robot execution success improvement from 40% to 95% and deployed the model on NVIDIA Orin through ONNX conversion with 0.5 s end-to-end latency.

- 项目 3：视觉车道级端到端定位 DiffVL
  - 时间：2024.01 - 2025.04
  - 角色：技术一号位；0-1 研发不依赖高精地图的端侧车道级定位模型
  - 成果：ICRA 2026 已录用

  - 中文 bullet 版
    - 0-1 负责 DiffVL 视觉车道级端到端定位模型，将视觉定位重塑为基于 Diffusion 的 GPS 轨迹去噪任务。
    - 联合建模 GPS 时序轨迹、UD 地图与视觉信号，通过时序注意力约束保证轨迹平滑、避免跳变，并通过逆向扩散逐步去噪含噪轨迹。
    - 构建多模态条件融合：图像特征转 BEV，UD 地图转拓扑表示，深度网络补全几何线索，三路条件共同约束扩散去噪过程。
    - 使用大模型合成夜间、阴雨等长尾困难场景图像，补齐训练分布并提升困难场景覆盖。
    - 三个分支均采用 MobileDet-0.25 骨干并结合量化，满足车机端实时推理。
    - 支撑高德车道级导航从 50 城覆盖扩展至全国，累计交付 280 万辆车机，并提升整体车道级定位准召。

  - English bullet version
    - Led 0-to-1 development of DiffVL, an edge-side lane-level visual localization model that reformulates localization as diffusion-based GPS trajectory denoising without relying on HD maps.
    - Jointly modeled GPS temporal trajectories, UD maps, and visual observations, using temporal attention constraints and reverse diffusion to denoise noisy trajectories while avoiding discontinuous jumps.
    - Built multimodal diffusion conditioning from image-to-BEV features, UD-map topology representation, and depth-network geometry completion.
    - Improved long-tail coverage by synthesizing night and rainy images with large generative models to fill difficult-scenario training gaps.
    - Adopted MobileDet-0.25 backbones and quantization across all three branches to meet real-time in-vehicle inference constraints.
    - Supported AutoNavi lane-level navigation expansion from 50 cities to nationwide coverage, with cumulative delivery to 2.8M in-vehicle units.

- 项目 4：云端视觉定位模型 VPS
  - 时间：2023.05 - 2023.12

  - 中文 bullet 版
    - 研发 Image-Retrieval 式视觉定位，从车机图像抽取 global descriptor，并与云端视觉底库进行 cross-matching。
    - 取 top-1 最相似图像对应 GPS 作为绑路结果，解决高架主辅路、立交、城市峡谷等复杂拓扑下的 GPS 错层与漂移。
    - 融合 SOCOL、猎鹰、ADAS、高铁等多源异构数据，适配多规格摄像头。
    - 采用 N-tuplets metric learning 降噪，并结合 GradCAM-based attention loss 与语义分割协同训练提升判别性。
    - 规模化落地高德打车派单、接送驾起点降偏航、抓路判偏等业务场景，降低起点偏航率与错绑路率。

  - English bullet version
    - Developed image-retrieval-based cloud visual localization by extracting global descriptors from vehicle images and cross-matching them against a cloud visual database.
    - Used the GPS of the top-1 matched image as the road-binding result to correct layer mismatch and GPS drift in elevated roads, interchanges, and urban canyons.
    - Adapted heterogeneous data from SOCOL, Falcon, ADAS, and high-speed-rail sources across multiple camera specifications.
    - Improved visual discriminability with N-tuplets metric learning, GradCAM-based attention loss, and semantic-segmentation co-training.
    - Deployed the system across AutoNavi ride-hailing dispatch, pickup deviation reduction, road-snapping diagnosis, and related point-line business scenarios.
